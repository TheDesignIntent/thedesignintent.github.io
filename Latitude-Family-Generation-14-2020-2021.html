Latitude Family Generation 14 2020-2021

Latitude Family of Products. Generation 14

I led the design program for the entire Dell Latitude commercial notebook family, spanning two product generations. This is a 50 billion dollar business for the company. My role was Design Director and creative lead, which meant I was accountable for the entire design output across a multi-disciplinary team of junior and mid-level designers working simultaneously across industrial design, research, and CMF alignment. This was not a single product or a focused sprint; it was a sustained, parallel-track program where the team was responsible for all design work across every tier of the Latitude portfolio, 9000, 7000, 5000, and 3000 series. From research inputs through final execution, at the same time.
The complexity of this program came from several directions at once. Covid interrupted the process and the program was extra complicated to execute and manage with half of my team in the US and the other half in Taiwan. Doing all of this while unable to travel with minimal in person meetings. We were managing multiple material platforms, CNC aluminum, stamped aluminum, cast magnesium, CFRP, and injection modled plastic. Each with its own manufacturing logic, surface behavior, and design constraints.
What that required of me as a leader was the ability to operate across every function without losing the thread of the program. I maintained design authority through the full ID-to-mechanical engineering translation, while co-developing product story and positioning with marketing. I presented the program to executives across design, engineering, planning, and procurement, and brought the customer's voice into the process directly through Dell's annual Customer Advisory Councils (they were virtual during this time). The discipline was keeping the design language coherent across ten systems and multiple material platforms while the team navigated that entire operational surface, not just the design work in front of them. This was a challenging effort, especially while trying to design through a computer screen. The team and I spent many late nights on zoom meetings with the manufacturing and development teams, working through all the details.

The approach for the team to get to the final Design.

Design reviews during covid were meetings held in random parking lots sharing models on the hood of our cars.

I made a lot of videos to share with decision makers during this time as well.
