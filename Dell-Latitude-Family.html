Dell Latitude Family

Latitude Family of products,12", 13", 14", and 15" systems. 2009.
Role: Team member and project leader.
This was a complete redesign of the Latitude family of products. Our goal was to introduce consumer trends into this category of product and start to bring in real materials to the family. This was a large team effort in this family of products. We spent many hours traveling to Asia to work with suppliers to work through all of the details of the system. Our process started by investigating current trends in business with the changing workforce and investigating a complete shift from the current design language of Latitude notebooks. A the time it was the largest single effort for a redesign of the Latitude family.
