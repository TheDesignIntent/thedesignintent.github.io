Latitude Z 2009

Dell Latitude Z 15" and 13" notebooks 2009
Role: Team member for 15" and project leader for 13" and accessories.
A product for the Digital Nomad. We were tasked with creating a true lifestyle product for an ultra mobile notebook in this space. This was a new effort for the Design team. This was a first for wireless charging for a notebook, wireless docking, blue tooth specific headset device for PC a phone device. We were offered many options to chose from and began investigating many different avenues for this program. This was an effort to touch every element of the ecosystem. All of the objects were designed with a fashion forward sense of style. During the development of this program the 13" system was cancelled as it was right during the 2008 recession. I don't know if we hit all the metrics we were trying to achieve. The product was launched right in the middle of a recession at a very high price point. We were trying to bring experiences to the system years before these types of experiences were being thought about. Even today more than a decade later some of these experiences we are still trying to achieve with little luck. The workflow with the team was one of the more enjoyable processes I had with a team of Designers. I have never made as many models for a program like this one. We left no stone unturned as we investigated experiences and finishes for this program. We wanted to make a true road warrior product they would be proud to carry.

15" notebook with wireless charging stand. This was an extruded piece of aluminum. We had to place the system up in the air for a couple of reasons. The wireless charging module was VERY thick and if we left it flat on the desk it would have been awkward to use. We felt that putting the display in the air like this we could create space for the custom kbd and place the display in a more ergonomic location for the user.
