Philips HD11 Ultrasound Machine

Philips HD11 and HD3 Ultrasound Machines
Role: Lead Designer
I was a new hire into Philips Design North American Design team. My role was to work with a newly acquired business by Philips. The development team used to be HP Medical. We had to harmonize the products to the rest of the Philips medical products. This was a good exercise in form development and patience with the development team. Being new to the process for this line of business was a challenge for me. Everyone was essentially new to Philips Design and the Philips Design process.
