Neon Belly Coffee and Bakery

Neon Belly Coffee and Bakery. Brand, Interior, and Experience Design for this business. Opened November 2025. Neon Belly started with a simple idea that a really great cup of coffee tastes even better in a room full of really great people.

Neon Belly started from a simple idea, a really great cup of coffee tastes better in a room full of people who want to stay. My wife Kat and I founded it together, and we designed every part of it ourselves, from the brand to the interior customer experience. From the menu layout to all of the recipes used.
Austin has a coffee shop on every corner. To stand out, Neon Belly couldn't just look good. It had to feel different the moment you walked in, and that feeling had to carry through the entire shop. It was a dichotomy. Fun bright colors contrasted to combat sports with the name of the business. IYKYK
I treated the café the way I treated product families, as one system, not a collection of parts. One design language, bold color, retro warmth, and a sense of play, runs through the identity, signage, packaging, and space. In the room, that shows up as swirling murals, neon, pink ottomans, a hexagonal tile counter, and a custom walk-up bar.

Kat and I led all of the design. For parts of the creative work, we brought in artists and an Architect and directed them so their work fit the larger system while still keeping their own voice. It was a long and collaborative process for us creating something really cool for Austin, TX.

Neon Belly opened on November 10, 2025. It's rated 4.8 stars from 115 reviews, was covered by CultureMap Austin and Community Impact, and has become a regular neighborhood spot on Burnet Road.
