Dell Precision Work Station Family 2014

Dell Precision Workstation Family.
Bringing enterprise class design to the workstation. I lead internal teams and an outside design agency in the design and development of these systems. Working with the internal project teams throughout the process. Directing development teams overseas in all aspects of the detailed development. I worked with the outbound marketing teams in explaining and crafting the story around the design intent and what the key elements of these products that are important to this type of customer. Making an airflow forward design and keeping with the continued idea of an airflow pattern that becomes the design. Making thermal flow a visible, intentional aesthetic rather than a concealed constraint. Directly addressing the sustained load performance demands of VFX, engineering, and data science workflows. Thermals are everything for these systems.
