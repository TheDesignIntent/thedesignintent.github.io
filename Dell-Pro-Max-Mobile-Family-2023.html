Dell Pro Max Mobile Family 2023

Dell Pro Max Mobile Workstations. Formerly Dell Precision.
I led the design program for the Dell Pro Max Mobile Family across six systems spanning Dell's flagship commercial workstation tier. This was end-to-end design ownership over Dell's most demanding product line, sitting at the top of the commercial portfolio above the Latitude family and competing directly against Apple, Lenovo, and HP at the premium workstation tier. Leading that program meant carrying accountability through every phase of the product lifecycle, navigating cross-functional reviews that included design, engineering, planning, and procurement leadership, and making design decisions that could withstand scrutiny at every level. I held design accountability through the full development cycle, from concept through engineering collaboration, DFM reviews, tooling, and production validation. At flagship tier, the gap between designed and manufactured is where a product either earns its position or concedes it. That required being present and decisive through each stage, resolving conflicts between industrial design intent and manufacturing constraints without compromising the standards the program was built around.
